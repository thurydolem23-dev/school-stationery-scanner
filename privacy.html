Privacy Policy

School Stationery Scanner is an inventory management application for authorized staff.

Information We Use

The application uses Google account authentication to identify authorized users.

Inventory transactions may record the staff member's name, email address, barcode, item information, quantity and transaction date.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to manage stationery stock, record inventory transactions and control access.

Data Storage

Inventory information is stored in an access-controlled Google Spreadsheet.

Camera Access

The camera is used to scan product barcodes. Barcode scanning does not require storing camera video.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the inventory administrator.
